About

I have completed my master's in Quantum Technology from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Pune (IISER Pune). My work is in experimental condensed matter physics, particularly two-dimensional materials, using Scanning Tunnelling Microscopy (STM) and Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM).
